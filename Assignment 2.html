About Kumbh Mela

Ancient Origin

Rooted in Hindu mythology, Kumbh Mela traces back to the legend of Samudra Manthan, the churning of the ocean for the nectar of immortality.

Massive Gathering

Held once every 12 years at each site, Kumbh Mela draws tens of millions of devotees, sadhus, and tourists from across the globe.

UNESCO Heritage

Recognized by UNESCO as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity for its unmatched scale and spiritual significance.

Four Sacred Locations

Haridwar
River Ganga

Gateway to the gods, on the banks of the holy Ganga in Uttarakhand.

Prayagraj
Triveni Sangam

Confluence of Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical Saraswati rivers.

Nashik
River Godavari

Held at Trimbakeshwar and Ramkund along the sacred Godavari.

Ujjain
River Shipra

Home of the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga on the banks of the Shipra.
